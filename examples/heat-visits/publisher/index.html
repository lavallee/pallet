Notes

Published 2024-06-01: Beginning with the 2024 report, a visit is counted when heat-related illness appears in any diagnosis field. Earlier reports counted a visit only when it was the primary diagnosis.

Counts below 5, and rates based on them, are suppressed and shown as *.

Lakeshore district was formed in January 2024 from the eastern part of Bayside. It has no 2023 figures.
